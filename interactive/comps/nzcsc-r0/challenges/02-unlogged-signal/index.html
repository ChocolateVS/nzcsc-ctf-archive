On a recent Monday evening, Carterton's mayor was out walking his dogs around 8.30pm when he spotted five or six oddly-behaved lights off toward South Wairarapa, roughly in the direction of Featherston and the Western Lake. By his account they moved up and down, side to side, and on the diagonal, fading in and out, hard to count, and clearly not an aircraft. His wife and daughter saw them too. He posted about it on the local community noticeboard, and the thread took off well over a hundred comments ranging from alien-invasion theories to comets and drones. A local astronomer offered the mundane read: Starlink satellites, low on the horizon, catching the last of the sunset before slipping into Earth's shadow. A similar light spotted a few days earlier turned out to be a rocket exhaust plume; a similar case the year before turned out to be a NASA research balloon launched from Wānaka. None of it was ever formally confirmed either way. In the middle of that same window, this exchange was picked up on an open frequency. It doesn't match any known transmission format. Two voices, handles only. Make of it what you will.
